Games — How to Play

Unlock games after your order is approved. Eligible orders need the account products set in admin (minimum quantity applies).

1

Spin the Wheel

Every approved eligible order earns one wheel slot. Wait for the draw date — if your slot wins, prizes are credited or shown on screen.

  • Buy the required account product (minimum quantity) from the shop.
  • After payment approval, your order number appears as a wheel entry.
  • When the draw runs, winners get loyalty credit, vouchers, or product prizes via Telegram.
2

Scratch Card

Scratch your card once per eligible approved order.

  • Open Games → Scratch Card and tap your card.
  • Reveal the prize — loyalty points credit automatically; vouchers show a copy button.
  • Product prizes: screenshot the win screen and send to our Telegram.
3

Mystery Box

Pick one box per eligible order — only one reveal counts.

  • Choose any mystery box on your play card.
  • Prizes follow the same rules: auto wallet, copy voucher, or Telegram screenshot for products.
4

Lucky Dice

Roll the dice once per eligible order.

  • Tap Roll — matching or winning combinations may award a prize.
  • Check the popup after each roll for claim instructions.
5

Card Flip

Flip one card per eligible order.

  • Pick a face-down card — your choice is final.
  • Win screen explains whether credit was automatic or needs a Telegram screenshot.
6

Treasure Vault

Try the vault once per eligible order.

  • Open the vault to reveal loot or a blank.
  • Same prize fulfillment: wallet, voucher code, or screenshot for physical/account prizes.
